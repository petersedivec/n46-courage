Quick summary

Ready to cross an ocean, or just the bay

Courage is a 2023 Nautitech 46 Open, built to the Explorer specification and delivered new from the Nautitech factory in Rochefort, France in May 2023. Owned and sailed by one family since new, she has crossed the Atlantic and spent a season cruising the Caribbean, with every engine hour, watermaker service, and maintenance action logged in detail from day one. She's currently hauled out at Hartman Cove, Grenada — a rare opportunity to buy a young, heavily-upgraded 46' cruising catamaran with a complete, documented history and no surprises.

She's set up to go anywhere: a 990Ah lithium bank with 2,550W of solar, a backup generator, a 60L/hr watermaker, 4-zone air conditioning, dual Starlink systems, and a full offshore safety inventory — all turn-key and ready to cruise. Full details on every system are on the Specs & Inventory page.
